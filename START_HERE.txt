SHORTS MAKER - private, free, runs only on your PC

1. Double-click  start.bat
   First time it sets itself up (a few minutes, needs internet): packages, FFmpeg, GPU boost.
   After that it starts in seconds and opens your browser at http://127.0.0.1:5055
2. Add a long video (or paste its file path), pick the language, press "Find the best moments".
3. Tick the clips you like, press "Make shorts", watch and download them.

Smarter clip picking (free): Settings -> paste a free Gemini key from https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
Without a key it still works using the offline picker.

First speech run downloads the AI speech model (about 1.6 GB for Balanced). Keep the window open.
Your videos and shorts stay in the "workspace" folder here.
